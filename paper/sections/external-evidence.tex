\section{Cross-source development on native REDS frames}
\label{sec:external-reds}
REDS was released for video deblurring and super-resolution~\cite{reds}.
We use its sharp validation source for a separate synthetic low-light
development check, without changing any reference weights. Clips 000,
009, 019 and 029 and their first 64 frames were fixed before pixel
acquisition. All 256 source frames retain their native $720\times1280$
resolution. The existing degradation applies a numerical sRGB power of
2.2, exposure factor 0.35, Poisson counts of 20 or 80, Gaussian standard
deviation 0.008, clipping and uint8 quantization, with JPEG disabled.
One registered PCG64 stream per source/condition is shared by all models.
This is not a calibrated RAW/ISP simulator or the official REDS task.

The five reference networks retain their completed 8,000-update
adaptations and both seeds. There is no REDS fine-tuning or checkpoint
selection. All 152 quality rows and 156 perceptual comparisons, including
four unique GT controls scoring 10, complete normally. The four videos
are the source units; two noise conditions and many frames do not add
independent scenes. Source-rate 24\,fps perceptual evaluation is kept
separate from the earlier nominal 30\,fps results.

MobileIE MSE recursion improves mean PSNR by 1.58--1.85\,dB at 20 photons
and 0.23--0.39\,dB at 80 photons across the two seeds. Temporal-error
ratios are 0.556--0.560 and 0.635--0.646, respectively. Yet two of its 16
source/condition/seed PSNR differences are negative, with the worst losing
0.284\,dB; all 16 JOD differences are positive. Strong recursion remains
necessary, without implying uniform per-source improvement.

The adapted spatial U-Net with recursion exceeds MobileIE MSE with
recursion by 1.356--1.833\,dB and 0.228--0.476 JOD across these four
condition/seed means. Its own recursion lowers temporal error in every
pair, but loses PSNR in six and JOD in five of 16 pairs. The largest
losses are 0.687\,dB and 0.176 JOD. Lower temporal error therefore does
not establish preserved image quality, even for a stronger spatial
reference. Capacity, public pretraining and training computation are not
matched by the common development updates.

Project adaptation uses the registered 57 DAVIS and 12 paired OLiVES
training source IDs, with no REDS source IDs. That does not establish
unseen public pretraining: author declarations and checkpoint/config
lineages are available, but public per-image training manifests and a
cross-source pixel audit are not. These four REDS sources have now been
used for development. They cannot be relabelled as a blind final test,
natural low-light evidence or a newly successful method.
