\section{A self-starting published causal reference}
\label{sec:causal-reference}
\paragraph{A learned-motion pilot does not supply the missing gain.}
The completed fixed motion-supervision pilot (R74) first freezes the
8K spatial mapping and trains two small motion predictors per seed,
with task and training-GT-derived transport targets respectively.
The four predictors receive 16K new updates in total. All 80 new
quality and 80 perceptual conditions complete, together with inspection
of all 20 preassigned contacts. The transport-target arm loses
1.93--3.13\,dB and 0.72--0.89 JOD relative to the spatial raw reference;
its temporal error remains 27.3--30.4\% above strong recursion.
All 16 joint screens fail. This fixed predictor/target setting is stopped
without a radius, teacher, width, loss-weight or length search. It does
not rule out other motion models or identify one cause of failure.

\paragraph{Released architecture, task-specific adaptation.}
ReBotNet~\cite{rebotnet} uses the previous enhanced prediction and
current input in a recurrent dual-branch bottleneck-mixer architecture.
We use its released XS configuration, with 890,275 parameters, without
altering the architecture. This adds a causal RGB reference without
future frames or an external deployment flow estimator. The released
code's token dimensions constrain its input shape. Native-resolution
deployment uses overlapping 384-pixel tiles, stride 256, short-edge
replication and normalized triangular blending; the full stitched,
unclamped prediction becomes the next history. Only reported outputs
are clamped. This changes context relative to a full-image network call.

Each seed starts from random initialization and receives exactly
64K Adam updates at learning rate $4\times10^{-4}$, cosine-decayed to
$10^{-7}$, using Charbonnier loss and gradient clipping at one.
Each update averages four four-frame videos from the fixed synthetic/real
mixture, with native 384-pixel crops and full four-frame backpropagation.
The first history is the current degraded input; no GT history,
teacher forcing or future observation is supplied. Both original runs
finish normally, for 128K new updates in total. Only their preassigned
64K final weights enter development evaluation. This is not the original
500K-update, high-quality-initial-frame paper setting, or a convergence
claim. MobileIE's 8K/128 training differs in capacity, pixels,
initialization and computation, so cross-network differences cannot
be assigned to architecture or history alone.

\paragraph{Full spatial, temporal and perceptual evidence.}

The causal output reads its own previous prediction. Current uses
the same trained weights with the current degraded input in both slots.
Self4 independently resets at each real frame and makes four complete
stitched calls, reusing only that frame's observation. Current changes
the history-slot distribution as well as removing past information;
self4 spends four times as many enhancement calls. Neither is an
independently trained same-capacity spatial network or a compute-matched
causal test. All first frames remain in the evaluation.

The primary pipeline completes 40 new quality and 40 JOD conditions,
each covering 1,792 frames. The supplement adds 20 conditions
and 896 frames without new training. All original 80 primary conditions
and eight screens remain unchanged in the combined 100-condition
collection. Table~\ref{tab:causal-reference} retains all 20 domain/seed
means, rather than selecting a favorable seed. SYN is six conditions
from three source videos at $480\times854$/64 frames; ND is four native
$288\times512$ crops/16 frames, not full 1080p. Both remain repeated
development evidence. All 20 primary and 20 supplemental fixed contacts
were actually inspected after rendering. Static first/middle/last views
do not constitute full-video playback, human MOS or a temporal study.

None of the eight primary screens passes
(Table~\ref{tab:causal-screens}). On SYN, causal loses 1.04--1.64\,dB
relative to strong recursion while temporal error is 60--62\% higher.
On ND it gains 4.13--5.33\,dB, but temporal error is still 23--53\%
higher. Relative to same-weight current, the temporal change ranges
from a 0.072\% increase to a 2.692\% decrease, below the registered
5\% improvement in every cell. Spatial recovery does not establish
useful historical information.

Only ND seed 1 passes the fixed self4 support check: its causal
temporal error is 13.38\% lower, with $-0.027$\,dB PSNR and
$+0.112$ JOD differences. The other three cells improve temporal
error by less than one percent. This isolated support does not rescue
the primary failures or establish a general causal advantage. The
pre-registered additional 128K independent-spatial-control investment
therefore stops; that control has not been trained. No width, unroll,
loss, learning-rate, length, seed or self4-pass grid is used to rescue
this setting. A failed adaptation does not invalidate the published
method or all recurrent enhancement models.

Inspection retains grain and smoothed textures, similar-looking
causal/current/self4 outputs in many contacts, and motion-boundary
blending in the classical recursive reference. Neither smoothness nor
lower temporal error alone proves better video quality. The XS
adaptation remains an ordinary reference, with no verified novelty,
handset measurement or independent final result. Its theoretical
1080p tiling uses 28 calls and 21.976G Conv/Linear MACs per output,
excluding other operations and memory work; it is not phone latency.
