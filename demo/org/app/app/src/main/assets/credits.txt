MobileIE Light Lab 0.2.0 — academic demonstration

MobileIE models and lowlight samples: MobileIE official repository
https://github.com/ChengxuLiu/MobileIE
Upstream Apache-2.0 license is included as model-license.txt.
Original lowlight TFLite bytes are retained. UIE/ISP use official frozen
PyTorch weights converted to FP32 TFLite; no training or weight tuning.

UIEB underwater samples: Chongyi Li et al., An Underwater Image Enhancement
Benchmark Dataset and Beyond, IEEE TIP 2020.
Dataset authors: https://li-chongyi.github.io/proj_benchmark.html
Distribution used: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Hikari0608/UIEB
The first three validation images are used for academic demonstration only.

Zurich RAW-to-RGB dataset: Andrey Ignatov et al., Replacing Mobile Camera
ISP with a Single Deep Learning Model, CVPR Workshops 2020 (PyNET).
Author source and dataset terms: https://aiff22.github.io/pynet.html
Three original Huawei P20 RAW test patches (0, 1, 10) are losslessly wrapped
as .rggb, without generated sensor data or value edits.

Exact source/asset hashes and transformations: task-asset-sources.json.
Data rights and terms remain with the respective dataset authors.
